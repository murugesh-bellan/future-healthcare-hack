Reflects autonomic nervous system balance and recovery capacity.

expand_more

"Further analysis suggests HRV trends are a leading indicator of systemic fatigue prior to perceived exertion."

Citation: "HRV as a marker of physiological stress" Journal of Applied Physiology, 2021 | n=1,240
Vocal Biomarkers

Subtle changes in vocal prosody can correlate with metabolic energy levels.

expand_more

Vocal analysis detects micro-fluctuations in frequency and rhythm that are often imperceptible to the human ear but highly indicative of metabolic strain.

Citation: "Acoustic features of speech in metabolic monitoring" Digital Health, 2022 | n=450
Grip Strength

A validated proxy for overall skeletal muscle vitality during weight loss.

expand_more

Consistent grip strength measurements ensure that weight loss is primarily coming from fat mass rather than functional muscle tissue.

Citation: "Muscle mass and strength during GLP-1 therapy" The Lancet Healthy Longevity, 2023 | n=890

What this isn't
